## Brand & Style
The design system manifests an ethereal sanctuary for metacognition, intellectual synthesis, and structured reflection. It serves philosophers, strategic thinkers, researchers, and system designers who require an interface that operates like a cosmic observatory for the mind.

The aesthetic fuses **Minimalist Editorial Luxury** with **Cosmic Glassmorphism**. The visual tenor is disciplined, sacred, and hyper-precise:
- **Atmospheric Depths:** Dark void backdrops layered with translucent obsidian glass evoke infinite spatial clarity without visual clutter.
- **Astral Illumination:** Restrained accents of Astral Gold appear like distant stellar coordinates, deployed deliberately to denote epiphanies, focal active states, and sovereign synthesis.
- **Architectonic Hairlines:** Sub-pixel borders and delicate grid lines reference astronomical astrolabes and classic typographics, providing quiet structural boundaries that elevate rather than overwhelm content.

## Colors
The color architecture reflects a high-contrast cosmic canvas that eliminates chromatic noise:

- **Primary (`#E2B96F` - Astral Gold):** Represents illumination, active cognition, and breakthrough states. Never used for dense background fills; strictly reserved for key CTAs, selected markers, aura glows, and editorial highlights.
- **Secondary (`#64748B` - Slate Gray):** Designates locked nodes, metadata, inactive lineages, and secondary contextual labels.
- **Neutral (`#090A0F` - Deep Obsidian):** The foundational substrate of the cosmos, absorbing visual strain and providing absolute depth.
- **Surface Obsidian (`rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.7)` / `#0E1118`):** Frosted mid-ground cards and contextual HUD panels, permitting underlying astral gradients to faintly bleed through.
- **Text Crisp White (`#F8FAFC`):** Delivers clean contrast against the obsidian background for high-priority philosophical prose and focal headers.
- **Boundary Hairline (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** Structural hairline definition used for card perimeters, dividers, and matrix axes.

## Typography
The system balances poetic contemplation with technical clarity through three distinct typographic roles:

- **Editorial Headings & Quotations (Playfair Display):** Infuses humanistic legacy and philosophical weight. High-contrast serifs ground major axioms, paradigm names, and reflective excerpts.
- **Interface & Prose Body (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Ensures uncompromised legibility across complex mental frameworks, interactive diagrams, and multi-paragraph synthesis.
- **HUD, Metrics, & Axiomatic Labels (JetBrains Mono):** Formatted in uppercase with extended letter spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`). Handles Roman numerals (e.g., `PARADIGM VII`), telemetry coordinates, timestamped insights, and system parameters.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 12-column adaptive fluid grid designed for spacious introspection:

- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `3rem` canvas margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Content blocks avoid edge-to-edge saturation; primary narrative threads maintain a max-width of `780px` to foster focused reading.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margins, `1.25rem` gutters. HUD panels collapse into sliding drawers or secondary overlay trays.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Technical tags stay pinned with persistent horizontal scrolling where necessary.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a geometric 8pt progression (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`), strictly separating data clusters without heavy card frames.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is expressed through atmospheric transmission and chromatic luminance rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - *Tier 0 (Abyss):* Base canvas `#090A0F`.
  - *Tier 1 (Stellar Glass):* `rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a `1px` border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
  - *Tier 2 (Floating Codex):* `rgba(20, 24, 34, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` and a `1px` border of `rgba(226, 185, 111, 0.2)`.
- **Ethereal Glow:** Active, selected, or focal nodes project an ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(226, 185, 111, 0.15)`.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** Rendered exclusively as `1px` hairlines with `background: linear-gradient(90deg, transparent, rgba(255,255,255,0.08) 50%, transparent)`.

## Shapes
A disciplined, architectural shape system (`0.25rem` standard radii) avoids casual toy-like curvature while preserving subtle refinement. 

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, crisp corners anchor the architectural discipline.
- **Editorial Callouts & Flyouts (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px), used sparingly for floating modal codices or system dialogs.
- **HUD Chips & Nodes:** Minimal `0.25rem` or sharp `0px` with chamfered geometry hints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Astral Action):** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid #E2B96F`, text `#E2B96F` in JetBrains Mono uppercase (`0.75rem`). Hover initiates a transition to `background: rgba(226, 185, 111, 0.1)` and an aura glow of `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(226, 185, 111, 0.2)`.
- **Secondary (Ghost Hairline):** Background `rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`. On hover: `border-color: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Mind-Nodes
- Obsidian glass container (`rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.7)`), hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), blur `16px`.
- Padding: `space-lg` (24px).
- Internal header uses Playfair Display (`headline-md`), accompanied by a JetBrains Mono Roman numeral indicator in `#64748B`.

### Input Fields
- Understated obsidian troughs with `background: rgba(9, 10, 15, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- Focus state: border transitions smoothly to `#E2B96F` with an inner glow `inset 0 0 8px rgba(226, 185, 111, 0.05)`. Placeholder text in `#64748B`.

### Chips & HUD Tags
- Compact height (`24px`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, background `rgba(14, 17, 24, 0.5)`.
- Typography: JetBrains Mono (`label-mono-sm`), uppercase with `0.1em` letter spacing.
- Inactive/Locked chips tint typography and border in `#64748B`. Active or epiphany chips transition border and text to `#E2B96F`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom square geometry (`16px x 16px`) with `roundedness: 1` (2px).
- Inactive: hairline border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`.
- Selected: border `#E2B96F`, containing an inner gold diamond or solid core with subtle aura illumination.

### Lists & Concept Hierarchies
- Connected by continuous `1px` vertical hairlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- Items reveal an Astral Gold indicator pip (`4px`) on hover, shifting the label color from `#64748B` to `#F8FAFC`.
